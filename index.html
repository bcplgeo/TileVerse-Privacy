🔒 Mannz Mahjong Privacy Policy

Developer: MannzVerse
Effective date: 21 November 2026
Contact: mannzverse.apps@gmail.com
In short: Mannz Mahjong needs no account and MannzVerse does not collect your personal information. Your progress stays on your device. The game is for all ages, so ads are set up to be suitable for children, are never personalised, and the app does not use the advertising ID.

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Mannz Mahjong ("the app"), a layered Mahjong solitaire game published by MannzVerse ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. Mannz Mahjong has no sign-in and does not access your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Level progress, stars, coins and best scores
  • Settings (music, sound, vibration and theme)
  • Whether you have bought "Remove ads", and a counter used to space out ads between levels

This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

4. Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen ad between levels (never during play) using Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. Because Mannz Mahjong is for all ages, every ad request is:

  • tagged as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as under the age of consent for EU/UK users;
  • limited to ads rated G (suitable for all audiences);
  • non-personalised: no interest-based advertising or remarketing.

The app does not use the Android advertising ID. To show ads, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address, device type, operating system and app version, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention, as described in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive your payment details; Google only confirms to the app that the purchase was made, so ads can be switched off and restored on reinstall. In the app, purchases and links that leave the game are protected by a simple grown-up check.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: to load ads and to complete or restore purchases.
  • Google Play Billing: to process the optional "Remove ads" purchase.

The game itself works offline.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), with Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy messages where required

These services are provided by Google and follow the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children's privacy

Mannz Mahjong is suitable for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are configured as described in section 4 in line with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us at any time and we will respond promptly.

9. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device under your control and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. No electronic storage is completely secure, but we do not hold any of your personal information on our own servers.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to delete all saved progress.
  • Buy "Remove ads" to stop ads between levels.
  • Where privacy laws give you rights (for example GDPR in the EU/UK or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in India), contact us and we will help. Because we do not collect personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features change. The effective date above will be updated, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
